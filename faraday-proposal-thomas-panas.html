Permits, HOA & local requirements
Local permitting and inspection requirements vary by city and county. It is the homeowner's responsibility to confirm whether a permit is required by their AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction).

Permit service is optional and listed as a separate line item in your estimate. If you request a city permit, Faraday Electric is responsible for obtaining the permit and coordinating all required inspections. If permit service is not selected, the homeowner assumes full responsibility for any permitting and inspection requirements.

Important: payment is required regardless of whether a city inspection has been completed. Inspection scheduling is handled by the city and may take days or weeks depending on availability — this is outside of Faraday Electric's control and does not affect the payment obligation.

Homeowners are responsible for complying with any applicable HOA rules or approval processes related to EV charger installations.
