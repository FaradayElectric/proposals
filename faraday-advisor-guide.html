Interactive Proposal — Advisor Guide

How to turn a CRM quote into a personalized interactive link you can text to a client — in about 90 seconds.

⚡ Why we do this

The cheap competitors text the client a price. We send an experience — their load calculation, a live diagram, annotated photos of their own home, our license & $2M insurance, and a charging-speed calculator for their car. Send this link with the quote, while they're still shopping — it's what makes us the obvious choice, not just one of the bunch.

What you need first

Before you open the tool, have these ready (your normal process already produces them):

  • ✅ The CRM quote — downloaded as a PDF
  • ✅ The load calculation — the Excel spreadsheet
  • ✅ The photos — satellite view, panel pictures, and the product renderings
  • ✅ The CRM "Accept" link for this client's quote

If you don't have all of it yet, wait — the link is only impressive when it's complete. (You can always send the link in two passes: quote-info first, photos when the renderings team finishes. See "Two-send option" at the end.)

The 7 steps

Step 1 — Open the editor

Open the proposal tool link (bookmark it). To open advisor mode, click the Faraday logo 5 times (or press Ctrl+Shift+E). The editor panel slides up. Clients never see this — it's locked on the published copies they receive.

Step 2 — Drop in the quote PDF
AUTO

In the green box at the top, click "or upload the quote PDF" and select the downloaded CRM quote. It auto-fills: customer name, address, phone, email, project #, charger, line items, wiring run, total price, and the scope note.

No PDF handy? Copy the quote text and paste it into the box, then click "Auto-fill from pasted text."

Step 3 — Upload the load calculation
AUTO

In the blue box, upload the load-calc Excel. It fills the panel rating, calculated load, breaker, and the full breakdown table the client can expand.

If the file has a subpanel tab, it asks: type 1 for main panel only (most jobs), or type 2 for main + subpanel.

Step 4 — Add the photos
AUTO + annotate

Scroll to "Project photos" and select all your images at once (satellite, panel, renderings). For each one you can add a caption and tap 📍 Annotate to drop labeled pins (e.g., "New Level 2 EV Charger", "Existing 200A Main Panel").

Step 5 — Set the two dropdowns YOUR CALL

These are judgment calls — the tool can't guess them:

  • Panel load situation — choose: Panel OK / Practical load (NEC 220.87) / Night schedule / DCC / SAK / Tesla Remote Meter / simpleSwitch / Panel Upgrade.
  • Subpanel situation — None / Subpanel required (new) / Use existing subpanel (closer) / Add-on. If you pick one, also fill the subpanel rating & load.
Step 6 — Review (the red checklist)
CHECK

At the top of the editor is a red "Review before you publish" list. Confirm the final price & permit total, the dropdowns, the panel numbers, the photos, the charger model, the client info spelling, and paste the CRM Accept link.

Step 7 — Publish & send

Click 🚀 Publish & get client link. If a non-default option (subpanel / load management) or a blank field needs a look, it'll ask you to confirm first. Then it shows the client link and a ready-to-send SMS — tap Copy SMS message, paste it into your texting app, and send.

Give the link about a minute to go live, then open it once yourself to confirm before texting the client.

New client? Hit Reset

To start a fresh proposal, open the editor and click 🔄 Reset for new client. It clears the previous client's data but keeps your advisor info and publishing setup.

One-time setup (per device)

The first time you use the tool on your computer, open ⚙ Publishing setup at the bottom of the editor and paste the GitHub username, repo name, and access token your admin gives you. It's saved in your browser — you only do this once.

⚠️ Always remember
  • Auto-fill is a head start, not the final word — review every field, especially the price.
  • Never label an AI/product rendering as the "final product" — it's an illustration until installed.
  • The link is a sales weapon for the shopping phase — send it with the quote, not after they've decided.

Two-send option (when renderings aren't ready)

Don't wait on the renderings team to send something impressive:

  1. Send 1 (with the quote): publish with everything you have now — quote info, load calc, plan, trust, terms. Text it with the quote.
  2. Send 2 (when photos are ready): reopen, add the satellite/panel/rendering photos, re-publish to the same link, and text: "Hi [name], just added the photos and engineering layout for your install — take a look 👀 [same link]"

Two touchpoints beat one — you're following up while the other contractors went silent.
