The plan, step by step

Installation at 19021 Bear Grass Drive Elgin Texas 78621. Next to Panel Out Side . All installations are completed in a single day.

1

Easy access, zero disruption

We only need access to your garage and the electrical panel — no access inside your home is required (unless your project includes attic/crawlspace runs or an interior panel). And the power stays ON the entire time: we never shut off electricity to your home during the installation. Bonus: if you can give us remote garage access (keypad code or smart opener), you don't even need to be home — total flexibility on scheduling.

2

New dedicated breaker

A 60-amp breaker is installed exclusively for your charger — nothing else shares this circuit.

3

Wiring & conduit

1 to 5 Ft — routed clean and to code.

4

Mount & connect

Your TWC is mounted next to panel out side , between 24in and 5ft above the parking surface per NEC 625.

5

Commissioning & walkthrough

We energize and commission your TWC, connect it to your Wi-Fi (when signal strength at the charger location allows), register it for warranty purposes, and walk you through everything before we leave.
